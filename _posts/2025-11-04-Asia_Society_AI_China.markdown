<hr class="page__divider">

November 4, 2025 — Asia Society Policy Institute's Center for China Analysis (CCA) Non-Resident Fellow on Chinese Society Barclay Bram is joined by Afra Wang, writer and researcher; Jessica Batke, senior editor for investigations at ChinaFile; and Yi-Ling Liu, author of the book “The Wall Dancers: Searching for Freedom and Connection on the Chinese Internet”, to unpack how artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping Chinese society.

The panel looks at how the specific conditions of the Chinese Internet; the platform economy, the impact of the surveillance and censorship apparatus, the interplay with hardware and a different sense of the existential risks associated with AI are playing out with the emergence of this new technology. The discussion also takes the temperature, quite literally, of some of the funkier AI applications in China, like mental health tools, why China has more AI boyfriends than girlfriends, and whether or not we should be excited about the prospect of AI-powered air-conditioning units. (1 hr.)
